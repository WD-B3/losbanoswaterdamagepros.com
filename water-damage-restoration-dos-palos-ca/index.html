When water is spreading in Dos Palos

If water is moving across a floor, the first minutes decide how far it travels. Work through the cards below in order, and call the 24/7 line as soon as you have done what is safe.

Stop the source

Close the shutoff at the fixture or the main water valve for the house. Water that keeps flowing undoes everything else.

Cut power only if it is dry and safe

Switch off power to wet rooms at the panel only if you can reach it on dry ground. Otherwise stay out and call the utility or 911.

Lift and photograph

Move small valuables and furniture legs off wet floors, then photograph the damage for your insurance claim.

Call the 24/7 line

Tell the dispatcher your street, the source and how far the water has spread. Same-day response is offered when a crew is available.

Ground, water and history in Dos Palos

Dos Palos was founded in the 1890s when Bernhard Marks persuaded cattle rancher Henry Miller to develop a town. Settlers who arrived in 1891 struggled to find good water, and many left in 1892. A second townsite, Colony Center, was renamed Dos Palos in 1906. The same account describes the land there as unsuited to farming and ranching in places because of swamps and unsettled soils.

That is history rather than a flood map, but it carries a practical lesson. Low ground and moist soil can keep crawl spaces and slab edges damp, so after any leak it is worth checking under the house. Our crawl space page and slab leak page explain what to look for, and the FEMA flood map for your address shows the official flood zone.
