AC Leak Water Damage in Los Banos, CA

Air conditioners remove moisture from the air, and when the drain backs up, that water ends up in your ceiling, wall or attic floor.

An air conditioner pulls moisture out of the air and sends it down a condensate drain. When that drain clogs or the pan cracks, the water has nowhere to go but into the ceiling, wall or floor around the equipment. This page covers AC leak water damage repair in Los Banos, the drying and repair that follow a backup.

If you see water near the unit, turn the system off at the thermostat so it stops making more condensate. A wet vacuum on the drain line can clear a simple clog, but call a heating and cooling technician if the pan has cracked or the line is blocked deeper.

Where AC condensate water goes

Where the equipment sits decides where the damage shows up. An attic unit sends water through insulation and into the ceiling below, a closet unit leaks into the floor and adjoining walls, and a rooftop or exterior unit can leak at the line set penetration. In each case the stain may appear far from the equipment.

Condensate water is generally considered clean, but it can carry dust, algae and biofilm from the drain pan. That is why wet insulation and drywall are removed if they stay wet, even when the water began as clean condensate.

Attic air handler

Overflow pan water soaks insulation and the ceiling directly below.

Closet or utility room unit

Water spreads to flooring and the base of drywall.

Window or mini split units

A tilted unit or blocked drain leaks into the wall below.

Ductwork and condensation

Sweating ducts can drip over time and stain the ceiling.

How AC leak damage is dried and repaired

The crew traces the moisture with a meter and thermal camera, removes saturated insulation, and opens the ceiling or wall only as far as needed. Air movers and a dehumidifier dry the cavity, and readings confirm when the structure is dry.

The HVAC technician repairs the drain, pan and float switch so it does not repeat. A float switch that shuts the system off when the pan fills is a low-cost addition to ask about.

Cooling loads in Los Banos

Air conditioners in the San Joaquin Valley work through long, hot stretches, so condensate drains and pans stay in service for months. Ask your HVAC technician to flush the drain line at the start of the cooling season, and look at the pan and float switch when the unit is serviced.

The City of Los Banos Building Division lists air conditioning work among the projects in its permit guidance. If a leak ends with a replaced air handler or relocated unit, contact the division at 520 J Street before installation so the work is documented.

What affects AC leak repair costs

Duration of the leak

A single overflow is a small job, while weeks of slow dripping soak insulation and framing.

Location of the unit

Attic units put water through insulation and a finished ceiling.

Insulation and drywall

Wet insulation and drywall are usually removed rather than dried.

Drying time

A closed cavity under insulation dries more slowly than an open room.

Equipment repair

The pan, line and float switch are repaired by an HVAC technician.

Common questions

Can an AC leak cause mold?

Moisture that stays in insulation or drywall can allow mold growth, which is why wet material is dried or removed promptly. Health questions about mold belong with a physician.

Is condensate water clean?

It starts as clean water, but it can pick up residue from the pan and drain. Material that stays wet is typically removed rather than trusted to dry.

Who fixes the drain line, you or an HVAC company?

An HVAC technician repairs the drain, pan and switch. Water damage restoration dries and repairs what the water reached.
