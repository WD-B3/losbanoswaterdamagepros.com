Water on the floor is an emergency, and the first hours decide how far it spreads. This is the page for water extraction in Los Banos, the job of pulling standing water out of a home or business with pumps and extraction units before drying begins. The dispatch line is open 24/7.

If water is still coming in, stop the source first. Close the main water valve if a supply line failed, keep people and pets out of the water, and lift small valuables off the floor. Then call and describe what you see, because the answers decide which equipment is sent.

When standing water is an emergency

Any standing water indoors is urgent, and it becomes more urgent the longer it sits. Water wicks up baseboards, soaks the edge of the subfloor and works its way under cabinets. The EPA says water-damaged areas and items should be dried in 24 to 48 hours to prevent mold growth, and extraction is the step that makes that window realistic.

After-hours calls are normal for this work. A washing machine hose that lets go at 2 a.m. or a water heater that fails on a Sunday does not wait for business hours, and neither does the dispatch line.

Electrical safety comes first

If water is touching outlets, a power strip, a panel or an appliance, stay out of the room. Cut power at the panel only if you can reach it while standing on dry ground. If you cannot, call the utility or 911 about the electrical risk before you call the restoration line.

What water extraction involves

Extraction is a short, physical job with a clear order. The crew confirms the source is off, identifies the water category, and removes the volume of water before any drying equipment is set. Taking out the bulk of the water first matters because drying equipment is slow and costly at moving liquid water.

Common questions

Do you handle water extraction emergencies at night or on weekends in Los Banos?

Yes. The line is answered 24 hours a day, including nights, weekends and holidays, and same-day response is offered when a crew is available. Tell the dispatcher where the water is, how deep it looks and whether any electrical items are wet, and shut off the water source if you can do so safely.

What is the difference between water extraction and drying?

Extraction removes liquid water with pumps and vacuums. Drying removes the moisture that has soaked into materials, using air movers and dehumidifiers. Both are needed, and extraction comes first.

How soon after a leak should extraction start?

As soon as the source is controlled. The EPA advises drying wet areas and items within 24 to 48 hours, so calling the same day gives the best chance of saving flooring and drywall.

Can I use a shop vacuum myself?

For a small spill on a hard floor, yes, if no electrical items are wet. For standing water over carpet, in more than one room, or near outlets, call a crew, because a household vacuum can leave pad and subfloor soaked.
