Carpet and the pad beneath it act like a sponge. This page covers carpet water damage restoration in Los Banos, from extraction and drying to deciding whether to clean, reinstall or replace.

Lift furniture off wet carpet, set aluminum foil or wood blocks under legs, and open windows or run a fan. Avoid walking on wet carpet since that pushes water deeper into the pad. Photograph the wet area before you start.

Can wet carpet be saved?

Three things decide it: the type of water, how long the carpet stayed wet, and the condition of the pad. Carpet that got wet from a clean supply line and is dried within a day or two can often be saved. Carpet soaked by sewage or floodwater is generally discarded, because it cannot be reliably sanitized.

As the EPA advises, water-damaged areas and items need drying within 24 to 48 hours to prevent mold growth, which is the same window professionals work to.

Clean water, fast action

Carpet is extracted, lifted if needed, and dried in place or after pad replacement.

Gray water

Carpet may be cleaned and dried, but the pad is usually replaced.

Category 3 water

Sewage and floodwater mean carpet and pad are removed.

Long-standing damp

Carpet that stayed wet for days is often replaced to avoid odor.

Carpeted rooms in Los Banos homes

Bedrooms and living rooms in many homes are carpeted over slab or raised floors. Over a slab, water can wick sideways into baseboards. Over a raised floor, it can drip through to the crawl space. Mention which you have when you call.

Renters should tell the landlord in writing about any wet carpet, with photos and the date. Carpet replacement is a common point of dispute, and a record protects you.

What to do before the crew arrives

Remove small items and furniture, and place foil or plastic under heavy legs to keep them from staining the carpet. Open windows if the air outside is dry, and run fans and a dehumidifier. Do not use a household vacuum on wet carpet.

Take photos and write down when the water started and where it came from. The source decides whether the carpet can be dried.

Signs wet carpet has a hidden problem

  • A musty smell that returns after drying.
  • Carpet that feels cool or damp days later.
  • Staining along the edges or baseboards.
  • Rippling that does not flatten after stretching.
  • Wet pad underneath when a corner is lifted.

If any of these show up, lift a corner and check the pad. Hidden moisture under carpet is what causes odor and mold weeks after a leak seemed to be over.

Common questions

How long can carpet stay wet before it must be replaced?

The EPA advises drying within 24 to 48 hours. Past that window, odor and mold risk rise, and replacement becomes more likely.

Do I need to replace the pad?

Usually yes. Pad soaks up water and dries slowly, and it costs little compared with the risk of odor and mold under good carpet.

Can you dry carpet without lifting it?

Sometimes, for a small clean water spill. For larger losses, lifting the carpet lets air reach the pad and subfloor, which dries them faster.
