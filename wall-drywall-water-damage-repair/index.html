Drywall is gypsum between sheets of paper, and it takes on water quickly. This page covers wall and drywall water damage repair in Los Banos, from drying a wet wall to cutting out and replacing sections that cannot be saved.

Signs include bubbling or peeling paint, a stain that grows, soft spots, a musty smell and baseboards that pull away. Stop the source of the water first, then photograph the wall and mark the edge of any stain with a pencil to see if it spreads.

Can wet drywall be saved?

Drywall that was wet briefly with clean water and has not softened can often be dried in place with the help of air movers and a dehumidifier. Drywall that is soft, crumbling, moldy or soaked by contaminated water is cut out and replaced.

Insulation behind the wall holds water against the drywall, so it is checked too. Wet batts are usually removed, since they dry slowly and keep the cavity damp.

Dry in place

Firm drywall with clean water is dried using air movers, dehumidifiers and small holes for airflow.

Flood cut

A cut a short distance above the water line removes the wet bottom and lets the cavity dry.

Full replacement

Soft or contaminated sheets are removed and new drywall is hung.

Finish

Taping, mud, primer and paint blend the repair into the wall.

Walls and drywall repairs in Los Banos

Before closing walls, the moisture readings should be dry. Closing wet walls is the main way a small leak turns into mold and odor months later. Ask for readings in writing, and keep them for your records.

Cutting and replacing large sections of wall is ordinary repair work, but structural changes may need a permit from the City of Los Banos Building Division at 520 J Street. Ask before opening load-bearing walls.

Signs a wall is wet inside

  • Paint that bubbles, peels or looks stained.
  • A wall that feels cool, soft or damp to the touch.
  • Baseboards that gap or lift at the bottom.
  • A musty smell near one wall.
  • Stains that return after being painted over.

Painting over a stain does not stop the moisture. Find the source, dry the wall and check the readings before you repaint.

What to do before the crew arrives

Move furniture away from the wall, take down pictures and open the area to air. Turn off the water supply if a pipe is involved. Mark the edge of the stain with a pencil and a date so it is easy to see if it is spreading.

Do not cut into the wall yourself near wires or pipes. A crew can open it safely and show you what is behind it.

Common questions

Do I have to replace all the wet drywall?

Not always. Firm drywall wet with clean water can often be dried in place. Soft, moldy or contaminated sections are cut out.

What is a flood cut?

It is a horizontal cut a short distance above the water line that removes the wet bottom section so the cavity can dry.

Will the paint match after repair?

A good finisher blends texture and paint across the repaired area. Matching older paint can need a full wall repaint.
