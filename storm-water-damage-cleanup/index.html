Storm water damage is rain or runoff that gets into a building through the roof, windows, doors, vents or low entry points. This page covers storm water damage cleanup in Los Banos, with 24/7 emergency response for homes and businesses.

If rain is coming through the ceiling, place a bucket, move belongings out of the way and keep clear of any ceiling that is bulging or sagging, since it can give way. Photograph the damage before you clean.

Storm water emergency: what to do first

Water entering from above or outside is urgent, but the first rule is safety. Stay away from standing water if any outlets or cords may be wet, and avoid flooded roadways or yards. If a tree or limb fell on the roof, keep out of the damaged area until it has been checked.

After the storm, a roofer can cover openings with temporary protection so more rain does not follow. Restoration then removes what got wet. The two jobs go hand in hand, and the sooner each starts, the less the water spreads.

Storm safety

Do not go onto a wet roof. Stay out of rooms with sagging ceilings, and keep power off at the panel if water is running through light fixtures and you can reach the panel while standing on dry ground. Call the utility or 911 if a power line is down.

Where storm water gets in

Roof and flashing

Missing shingles, worn flashing and plugged gutters let rain into attics and ceilings.

Windows and doors

Wind-driven rain can pass worn seals and soak walls and floors beneath them.

Grade and foundations

Water that pools against a foundation can seep through slabs or into crawl spaces.

Drains and sewers

Heavy runoff can overload drains, so water backs up into low fixtures.

How storm water cleanup works

The crew traces water with moisture meters and a thermal camera, removes standing water, and takes out soaked insulation, carpet pad and drywall that will not dry in place. Air movers and dehumidifiers then dry what remains.

Because outside water may carry soil and other contaminants, the water category matters. It can change what can be dried and what is removed.

Before the rainy season, look at your roof, gutters and drainage. The Merced County Office of Emergency Services posts storm alerts and sandbag locations, and the City of Los Banos shares updates on its website.

After the rain stops

Once the storm passes, walk the property in daylight. Look for stained ceilings, wet walls near windows, damp carpet at exterior doors and pooled water along the foundation. Clear gutters and downspouts, and move soil or debris away from the house so water drains away from the walls.

Keep receipts for any temporary repairs. Insurers generally expect reasonable steps to prevent further damage, and your photos and receipts show what was done.

What affects storm water cleanup costs

Entry point

A roof leak over one room differs from a flooded ground floor.

Water category

Rain through a roof is cleaner than runoff from the yard or a backed-up drain.

Duration before help

Water that sits through a weekend soaks further than water removed in hours.

Materials affected

Insulation, drywall and carpet are common removals.

Roof or exterior repair

Roofing, siding and window work is a separate trade and cost.

Common questions

Do you handle storm water damage emergencies at night or on weekends in Los Banos?

Yes. Storms do not arrive on a schedule, so dispatch answers through the night and on every holiday. Same-day response is offered when a crew is available. Place buckets, move belongings away from leaks, and call.

Is rain damage covered by homeowners insurance?

Rain entering through a storm-damaged roof or opening is often covered, while flood from rising water usually is not. Your policy and your insurer decide.

Should I tarp the roof myself?

Do not climb a wet roof. A roofer can place temporary protection safely, and restoration handles the water inside.

How fast should wet insulation be removed?

Soaked insulation holds water against the ceiling and does not dry well in place, so it is usually removed during the first visit.
