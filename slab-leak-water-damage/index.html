Slab leak water damage starts with a pipe that runs inside or beneath a concrete floor. Water from that pipe moves up through cracks and joints, soaks flooring from underneath, and often shows no puddle at all. This page covers the cleanup and drying after a slab leak, for homes in Los Banos, with 24/7 availability.

If you suspect one, close the main water valve and check whether the meter keeps moving. Move rugs and furniture off the damp flooring and take photos of the warm spot, the damp baseboard or the wet carpet edge.

Warm floor, running meter, damp carpet with no leak in sight?

Many people never use the words slab leak. They search for a warm spot on the floor, a water meter that keeps running with everything off, the sound of water running when no tap is open, or carpet that is damp along one wall. Those are the symptoms of a leak in a line under the concrete, and the trade name for finding and drying it is slab leak water damage cleanup.

Detecting and repairing the line itself is plumbing work, often with acoustic or thermal tools. The cleanup side removes what water has soaked and dries the structure above and around the slab.

Slab leak emergency: when to call right away

Treat water coming up through floors, a hot spot on a floor, or a meter running with the main off as urgent. Even if the amount looks small, water under flooring can soak the subfloor and walls above the slab. Hot-water lines can also raise floor temperature and cause scald risk where water surfaces.

Do not stand on warm, wet flooring near electrical items. If the leak is near outlets or the panel, cut power only if you can reach it dry, or call the utility or 911 about the electrical risk first.

Baseboards, carpet edges and wall bottoms that stay damp point to a source below.

Sound

Hissing or running water behind walls with all fixtures off deserves a plumber's check.

What slab leak cleanup involves

The plumber locates and repairs the line, which can mean opening the slab or rerouting the pipe overhead. The restoration crew extracts water, removes flooring or baseboards that cannot be saved, and sets drying equipment on the slab and the lower wall.

Concrete and the soil beneath it dry slowly, so drying is tracked with readings rather than guessed. The work is documented for your insurer, including photos of any removed flooring.

Slab foundations and Los Banos homes

Whether a leak can happen under the floor at all depends on how your home was built, so find out what yours sits on. Your insurer or the county assessor's records can tell you. Homes with crawl spaces instead belong on the crawl space page.

Water bills tell the story early. The City of Los Banos bills for water through its Finance and Billing department, and a sudden jump in usage with no change in habits is a prompt to run the meter test. Mention the bill history when you call.

Common questions

Do you handle slab leak emergencies at night or on weekends in Los Banos?

Yes. When water is coming up through a floor or the meter is spinning with everything off, the line is answered at any hour, including nights, weekends and holidays. Same-day response is offered when a crew is available. Close the main valve and call.

How do I know if I have a slab leak and not a surface leak?

A running water meter with all fixtures off, a warm floor patch, or damp flooring along a wall with no visible source suggest water coming from below. A plumber can confirm with detection tools.

Can you fix the pipe under the slab?

Pipe repair or rerouting is plumbing work. Restoration removes the water and dries the structure, and we coordinate timing with your plumber.

Does the water bill matter?

Yes. Utility bills show sudden usage jumps, which help confirm a hidden leak and are useful for your insurance file.
