If water is still spreading in Newman

Shut off the supply

Turn the fixture valve or the main water valve clockwise. If you cannot find it, turn off the supply at the meter box if you can reach it safely.

Keep power and water apart

Do not stand in water near cords or outlets. Cut power at the panel only from dry ground, or call the utility or 911.

Protect what you can lift

Raise furniture legs onto blocks or foil, pull rugs and boxes off the floor and photograph everything for the claim.
