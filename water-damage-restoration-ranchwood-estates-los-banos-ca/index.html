Ranchwood Estates is a residential neighborhood in Los Banos with its own street name, Ranchwood Drive. A neighborhood listing site says the community dates to 1964 and that homes there were built between 1964 and 2001. It describes mostly midsize single-family homes of about 1,070 to 2,981 square feet, with 3 to 5 bedrooms.

Those dates are the useful part. A house from the 1960s and a house from the 1990s can sit a few doors apart and have different supply lines, water heaters and wall finishes. Call (831) 271-0459 and we will ask what you can see before we plan the work.

Common leaks in a mixed-age neighborhood

Aging water heaters

A tank at the end of its life can leak into a closet or garage. See water heater leak cleanup.

Supply line failures

A burst or loosened line can wet a floor quickly. See burst pipe cleanup.

Appliance hoses

Washers, dishwashers and ice makers can drip behind or under the unit. See appliance leak damage.

Ceiling stains

A stain can come from a roof leak or a pipe above. See ceiling water damage repair.

We do not assume which problem you have. A moisture meter and a look at the source tell us more than the age of the house does.

Safe first steps

If water is dripping through a light fixture or ceiling, keep the circuit off only if you can reach the panel on dry ground. Otherwise stay clear and call the utility or 911.

Close the shutoff at the fixture or the main water valve. Do not use a household vacuum on standing water.

Extraction and drying, step by step

Crews first remove standing water, then dry what is left behind. Carpet and padding are checked for salvage, and wall cavities are measured before anything is cut open. The carpet page and structural drying page explain the choices.

The EPA advises drying water-damaged areas and items within 24 to 48 hours. If a leak went unnoticed for days, tell us when you first saw a stain or smelled dampness.

Documenting a loss for your insurer

The California Department of Insurance says flood damage is typically excluded from standard homeowners policies and needs separate coverage. Sudden pipe or appliance failures are a different question, and only your insurer can answer it for your policy.

Take photos before anything is moved, keep the failed part and save receipts. We provide moisture readings and a written scope. Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status not verified.
