What to avoid in the first hour

Do not stand in water and operate switches or breakers. If outlets are wet, leave the panel alone and call the utility or 911.

Do not run a household vacuum on water, and do not try to remove sewage yourself. Contaminated water needs protective gear and a controlled cleanup.

Drying a larger home properly

A bigger house often means more rooms downstream of one leak. Water can travel along floors, into baseboards and under walls into the next room. We measure the edges of the wet area, remove standing water and then set drying equipment for the whole affected path.

Speed matters here too, since the EPA points to a 24 to 48 hour window for drying water-damaged areas and items. Our water extraction and structural drying pages show how the two stages work.
