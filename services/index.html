Water Damage Services in Los Banos, CA

Every water loss starts somewhere, and the right page depends on where. This index groups our services by cause, from a burst pipe or a failed appliance to a storm leak, a sewage backup or a wet floor. Each page explains the signs, the steps, the cost factors and the insurance questions for that type of loss.

If you are not sure which page fits, call the 24/7 line and describe what you see. The dispatcher will point you to the right next step.

Pipes and plumbing

Supply lines, drain lines and water heaters are the source of most indoor water losses. These pages cover the emergency steps and the cleanup that follows.

  • Burst pipe water damage for a pressurized line that has let go.
  • Frozen pipe water damage for pipes that freeze and split.
  • Water heater leak cleanup for tank failures and slow leaks.
  • Slab leak water damage for hidden leaks under the floor.

Appliances and HVAC

Machines that use water can leak into cabinets, floors and ceilings. Start here if the water came from a washer, a dishwasher, a refrigerator line or an air conditioner.

  • Appliance leak water damage for washers, dishwashers and refrigerators.
  • AC leak water damage for clogged condensate lines and pans.
  • Kitchen water damage restoration for cabinets, floors and walls.

Weather and building envelope

Rain, runoff and rising water enter through roofs, walls, doors and the ground. These pages cover storms, floods and the low spaces where water collects.

  • Ceiling water damage repair for stains, sags and drips.
  • Storm water damage cleanup for roof, window and runoff intrusion.
  • Flood damage cleanup for rising water and contaminated flooding.
  • Basement water damage cleanup for below-grade rooms.
  • Crawl space water damage for water under the house.

Water removal and drying

Once the source is stopped, water has to come out and the structure has to dry. These pages explain the core steps and the contaminated-water case.

  • Water extraction for pumps and extraction units.
  • Structural drying and dehumidification for walls, floors and framing.
  • Sewage backup cleanup for Category 3 water.

Floors and walls

Floors and walls take the damage that most people see. These pages explain what can be saved and what is replaced.

  • Hardwood floor water damage repair for cupping, buckling and staining.
  • Carpet water damage restoration for carpet and pad.
  • Wall and drywall water damage repair for stained and soft walls.

Commercial

  • Commercial water damage restoration for shops, offices, restaurants and warehouses.

Not sure which page fits?

Water damage rarely fits one box. A burst pipe becomes a wet ceiling and a soaked carpet, and a storm leak becomes a wall problem. Start with the cause, since stopping the source comes first, and then follow the links on each page to the related repairs.

Our blog has guides on insurance, costs, drying time and preparing for storms. For towns outside Los Banos, see the service area page, or the pages for Dos Palos and Newman.
